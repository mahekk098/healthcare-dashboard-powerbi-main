# Healthcare Analytics Dashboard - Papollo Healthcare

An interactive, multi-page Power BI analytics solution designed to provide healthcare executives, operational leaders, and clinical directors with end-to-end insights into patient demographics, clinical performance, resource utilization, and financial revenue.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Papollo Healthcare Analytics Dashboard** processes healthcare operational data across 8 major branch cities from **January 2023 to March 2024**. It translates multi-departmental metrics into actionable insights to streamline hospital operations, optimize physician workload, monitor bed occupancy, and maximize revenue efficiency.

---

## 📊 Executive Summary & Key Metrics

* **Total Patient Count:** 7,157 Patients
* **Total Gross Revenue:** ₹19.04 Crore (190 Million)
* **Average Billing per Patient:** ₹26,608
* **Emergency Admission Rate:** 60.01%
* **Average Length of Stay (ALOS):** 8.25 Days
* **Private Bed Occupancy:** 3,579 Patients
* **Insurance Provider Network:** 6 Active Payer Partners

## 📑 Dashboard Architecture & Page Breakdown

### 1. Analytics Dashboard Overview (Cover Page)
* **Purpose:** High-impact landing page highlighting core business KPIs.
* **Featured Metrics:** Total Patients (7,157), Total Revenue (190M), Emergency Cases Ratio (60%), and Cities Covered (8).

---

### 2. Executive Overview Page
* **Purpose:** Strategic command view for C-suite leaders to track organizational performance trends.
* **Key KPI Cards:** Total Patients, Total Revenue, Emergency Admission %, Average Length of Stay, Male vs. Female Split, Private Bed Count, and Active Insurance Count.
* **Monthly Admissions Trend:** Continuous timeline visual (Jan 2023 – Mar 2024) illustrating patient intake cycles, seasonal surges, and volume shifts.

---

### 3. Patient Demographics & Regional Distribution Page
* **Purpose:** Geographical and demographic profiling to identify target patient segments and regional demand.
* **City-Wise Analysis:** Analyzes patient distribution across 8 cities: Navi Mumbai (925), Pune (922), Aurangabad (915), Thane (895), Mumbai (885), Nashik (876), Solapur (873), and Nagpur (866).
* **Age Group Segmentation:** Breakdown by age groups, led by Young Adults (1,148), Early 60s (607), Late 30s (587), down to Early 80s (113).
* **Gender Split:** 67.11% Male (4,803) vs. 32.89% Female (2,354).
* **Admission Classification:** 60.01% Emergency cases (4,300) vs. 39.99% Scheduled cases (2,860).

---

  * Pneumonia (600 cases)

* **Bed Occupancy:** Allocations across room types: Private Ward (3,600), General Ward (2,400), and ICU (1,200).
* **Doctor Workload:** Monitors patient load across attending doctors (Jay Sinha, Jaya Yaadav, Mark Joy, Naresh Goyenka, Niki Sharma, Ravi D, Tejas Saxena) ensuring balanced caseload management (~1,022 to 1,023 patients per physician).

## 💡 Key Business Takeaways & Recommendations

1. **Capacity Management:** High demand for Private beds (3.6K) suggests potential for expanding premium room offerings.
2. **Emergency Preparedness:** Emergency cases represent 60% of all admissions, highlighting the need for strong triage and emergency response resources.
3. **Payer Network Optimization:** Star Health and HDFC Ergo constitute the largest insured patient volume; optimizing claim processing workflows with these providers can shorten reimbursement cycles.
4. **Physician Allocation:** Doctor workloads are evenly balanced, demonstrating efficient allocation strategies across the medical team.
